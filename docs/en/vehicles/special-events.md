# Special Events & Limited Vehicles

VoltFracht features time-limited events and exclusive collector vehicles that
are only available during specific periods. These special events introduce
unique cargo types, exclusive trucks, and competitive leaderboards.

---

## Halloween Special Event

**Duration**: 03 October 2026 - 31 October 2026

The Halloween Special is a seasonal event featuring unique cargo and limited
edition vehicles with special bonuses.

### Pumpkin Cargo

* **Availability**: Only available as 7.5 t and 18 t orders
* **Payout Rule**: 40 t trucks can deliver pumpkin cargo but receive the
  same 18 t payout as smaller vehicles (no size advantage)
* **Leaderboard**: Dedicated weekly rankings with rewards for all participants

### Spectre 7.5 t 2026

**Availability**: 03 October 2026 - 31 October 2026

The Spectre 7.5 t 2026 is an exclusive Halloween-themed light distribution
truck available only during the seasonal event.

* **Purchase Price**: 2'599 Coins (★)
* **Market Value**: €150'000
* **Energy Usage**: 61 kWh/100 km
* **Battery Capacity**: 205 kWh
* **Max Charging Power**: 180 kW
* **Range**: 336 km
* **Special Bonus**: Double points for pumpkin cargo
* **Availability**: Available for Coins only, once per account. After the event
  concludes, can only be obtained through the Used Truck Marketplace

---

### Spectre 18 t 2026

**Availability**: 03 October 2026 - 31 October 2026

The Spectre 18 t 2026 is an exclusive Halloween-themed medium haul truck
available only during the seasonal event.

* **Purchase Price**: 5'999 Coins (★)
* **Market Value**: €250'000
* **Energy Usage**: 104 kWh/100 km
* **Battery Capacity**: 500 kWh
* **Max Charging Power**: 250 kW
* **Range**: 481 km
* **Special Bonus**: Double points for pumpkin cargo
* **Availability**: Available for Coins only, once per account. After the event
  concludes, can only be obtained through the Used Truck Marketplace

## Early Bird 7S Collector Vehicle

**Availability**: 30-day limited release starting with Version 0.15

The Early Bird 7S is a prestigious collector e-truck available only during
a special launch window.

* **Nominal Market Value**: €150'000
* **Purchase Method**: Available for Coins (★) only, once per account
* **Energy Usage**: 60 kWh/100 km
* **Battery Capacity**: 210 kWh
* **Max Charging Power**: 175 kW
* **Range**: 359 km
* **Availability Period**: 30 days from initial release (now expired), then
  permanently removed from the shop
* **Rarity**: After the window closes, the Early Bird 7S can only be obtained
  through player-to-player trading on the Used Truck Marketplace
* **Market Status**: As a rare collector's item, its market value on the
  Used Truck Marketplace is determined by supply and demand

---

## Event Participation Tips

* **Plan Ahead**: Ensure you have available depot slots before events start
* **Monitor Announcements**: Event start dates and durations are announced
  through official channels
* **Check Marketplace**: After events conclude, limited vehicles may appear on
  the Used Truck Marketplace at premium prices
* **Join Syndicates**: Participating in a syndicate enhances event rewards
  through collective achievements
