# Vehicle Classes & Specifications

VoltFracht features five distinct electric truck classes, each designed for
specific logistics roles across European transport networks. All vehicles
operate autonomously once dispatched, with route planning, charging stops, and
delivery execution handled automatically.

---

## Truck Class Overview

| Class | Gross Weight | Ideal Role | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **7.5 t** | 7.5 tonnes | Light Distribution | Urban delivery & regional starter runs |
| **18 t** | 18 tonnes | Medium Haul | Regional distribution & event transport |
| **26 t** | 26 tonnes | Heavy Rigid | Inter-city logistics & medium haulage |
| **40 t** | 40 tonnes | Articulated Semi | Long-distance cross-border logistics |
| **44 t** | 44 tonnes | Articulated Heavy Semi | Long-distance cross-border heavy logistics |

---

## Class-Specific Characteristics

### 7.5 t - Light Distribution

* **Primary Use**: Ideal for new dispatchers and urban operations
* **Advantages**: Lower purchase cost, better fuel efficiency per tonne
* **Best For**: Local deliveries, short regional routes
* **Unlock**: Available from start with default cargo licenses

### 18 t - Medium Haul

* **Primary Use**: Regional transport and event-specific cargo
* **Advantages**: Balanced capacity and operational cost
* **Best For**: Inter-city routes, event cargo (e.g., pumpkin during Halloween)
* **Unlock**: Requires tonnage class upgrade

### 26 t - Heavy Rigid

* **Primary Use**: Intermediate haulage between cities
* **Advantages**: Higher payload capacity for mid-range distances
* **Best For**: City-to-city logistics, medium-distance runs
* **Unlock**: Requires tonnage class upgrade

### 40 t - Articulated Semi

* **Primary Use**: Long-distance cross-border logistics
* **Advantages**: High cargo volume for international routes
* **Note**: For pumpkin cargo during Halloween Special, receives same 18 t
  payout (no advantage over smaller classes)
* **Unlock**: Requires tonnage class upgrade

### 44 t - Articulated Heavy Semi

* **Primary Use**: Maximum capacity long-distance haulage
* **Advantages**: Largest payload for cross-border heavy transport
* **Best For**: Heavy logistics, maximum revenue long-haul routes
* **Note**: For pumpkin cargo during Halloween Special, receives same 18 t
  payout (no advantage over smaller classes)
* **Unlock**: Requires tonnage class upgrade

---

## Vehicle Performance Factors

All truck classes are affected by the same core performance variables:

* **Payload Weight**: Heavier cargo increases energy consumption per km
* **Topography**: SRTM elevation data affects battery drain on climbs and
  recuperation on descents
* **Weather**: Temperature cycles impact battery efficiency and HVAC power draw
* **Charging Infrastructure**: Plug availability and queue times affect route
  duration
* **Energy Costs**: ENTSO-E spot prices vary by country, affecting margins
