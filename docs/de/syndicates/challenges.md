# Syndicates & Weekly Challenges

In **VoltFracht**, dispatchers can pool their operational resources by 
forming or joining a **Syndicate** (*Syndikat*). Syndicates allow up to 10 
independent haulage companies to coordinate fleet operations, complete 
collective weekly challenges, manage shared alliance treasuries, and compete 
across 17 promotion leagues.

---

## 1. Alliance Structure & Member Roles

Every syndicate functions as an alliance of independent transport companies 
collaborating toward shared milestones and leaderboard standings.

### Alliance Capacity
* **10-Company Cap**: Each syndicate can host a maximum of **10 haulage 
  companies**.
* **Public Directory & Applications**: Open syndicates are listed in the 
  global directory. Dispatchers can review syndicate statistics, total 
  fleet size, and member profiles before applying.

### Management Roles
| Role | Title in Game | Core Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Leader** | *Leitung* | Manages member invitations, approves applications, withdraws pending invites, and configures alliance settings. |
| **Treasurer** | *Kassenwart* | Oversees shared alliance savings goals (*Sparziele*), manages community treasury funds, and tracks member contributions. |
| **Member** | *Mitglied* | Contributes fleet mileage, completed jobs, and earnings toward weekly challenges and shared savings pools. |

---

## 2. Weekly Challenges & Score Mechanics

Every week, syndicates receive **four distinct weekly challenges** designed 
to test various operational aspects of their combined fleets:

1. **Electricity Consumed** (*Strom geladen*): Total kilowatt-hours 
   charged across all alliance e-trucks.
2. **Total Revenue** (*Umsatz*): Combined gross earnings in virtual 
   Euros (€) generated by completed freight orders.
3. **Distance Covered** (*Kilometer*): Total distance in kilometers 
   driven across European road networks.
4. **Orders Delivered** (*Aufträge*): Total volume of successfully 
   completed delivery contracts.

### Dynamic Fleet Scaling

To ensure fair competition between small startups and established fleets, 
weekly target thresholds scale dynamically using the following formula:

```
Target = Base Value per Truck × Total Syndicate Truck Count × League Multiplier
```

This scaling mechanism ensures that a young syndicate with 2 players 
operating 3 trucks each faces achievable targets relative to a full 
10-company alliance operating maxed-out 12-truck fleets.

### Scoring Math: Leaderboard vs. League Promotion

* **Target Percentage**: Completing 100% of a challenge target yields 100 
  score points.
* **Over-Fulfillment (Global Leaderboard)**: Points continue to accumulate 
  past 100% without cap (e.g., reaching 430% of an electricity goal yields 
  430 points). The total weekly score is the sum of all four un-capped 
  percentage scores.
* **Capped Promotion Points (League Standing)**: For league promotion 
  calculation, each challenge score is capped at 100%. This prevents 
  massive alliances from monopolizing promotion slots purely through raw 
  vehicle volume, keeping league movement competitive.

---

## 3. The 17 Promotion Leagues

Syndicates compete within a structured ladder system consisting of **17 
promotion and relegation leagues**.

* **Weekly Settlement**: League standings are evaluated at the conclusion 
  of each weekly cycle.
* **Promotion & Relegation**: Top-performing syndicates in each league 
  earn promotion to the next higher tier, while lower-ranking alliances 
  risk relegation.
* **League Multipliers**: Higher leagues feature steeper target multipliers 
  but yield enhanced weekly prestige bonuses, cash distributions, and Coin 
  rewards for all participating members.

---

## 4. Shared Savings Goals & Treasury (*Sparziele*)

In addition to competitive weekly challenges, syndicates can establish 
shared financial savings goals (*Sparziele*):

* **Collective Contributions**: Members can deposit virtual capital into 
  the syndicate treasury in a single transaction (*Spenden in einem Rutsch*).
* **Tiered Reward Distribution**: When a savings goal is met, rewards 
  (including cash, Coins, and prestige points) are distributed to all 
  contributing members proportional to their share of total donations 
  (*Spendenanteil*).
* **Expanded Treasury Capacity**: Updated syndicate features support 
  expanded donation caps and real-time push notifications when savings 
  milestones are unlocked or completed.
